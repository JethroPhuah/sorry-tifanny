Your list is three trips wearing one coat. The honest plan picks which one to wear.

The pins fall into four driving clusters that are up to six hundred kilometres apart. Nara’s mountains, the Tango coast and Lake Biwa each make a fine day out of Osaka. The four Alpine places, Kamikōchi, Norikura, Hirayu and Hirugano, sit five hours north and two of them ban private cars, so they need two nights in the hot-spring village of Hirayu. Below the atlas you choose: the Alps loop your list was really pointing at, or three day trips that sleep at home.

Silver Week falls on 19–23 September 2026. Respect for the Aged Day, a bridge holiday and the Autumnal Equinox line up into a five-day national holiday. Rental prices roughly double and Kamikōchi shuttles queue for an hour. If your dates can move, drive on 8–11 or 14–17 September.

The car

Cheap, automatic, picked up close to Hōshin.

Every rental car in Japan is automatic unless you ask for manual. The saving is in the class you pick and the desk you book through. Book a compact hybrid: it is ¥1,500 a day more than a kei car and the only one of the two that climbs to Hirayu with luggage without complaint.

Bring to the counter
  • Passport, home licence, and a 1949 Geneva Convention International Driving Permit. Singapore (AA Singapore, same day) and Malaysia (JPJ) permits are accepted.
  • Licences from Germany, France, Switzerland, Belgium, Monaco, Taiwan, Slovenia or Estonia need a JAF Japanese translation instead.
  • Ask for an ETC card: 30 percent off weekend tolls and the only way through the Hirugano smart interchange.
  • Add the NOC waiver, about ¥550 a day. Without it a scratch costs ¥20,000–50,000 on top of insurance.
  • Return with a full tank (“mantan”) and keep the receipt.

Parking and the bill

Where the car sleeps, and what three days cost.

  • Overnight near Hōshin. Coin parking around Kami-Shinjō and Awaji stations caps at ¥800–1,200 a night; look for 夜間最大 on the sign. The akippa app pre-books private spaces from about ¥500 a day. Ask about the building’s visitor bay first.
  • Plan B keeps the car three days and parks at home. Returning to the desk each night wastes more time than the parking costs.
  • Mountain lots are free or nearly. Ōdaigahara, Hirayu Falls, Honokidaira and Makino Pic-Land are free; Akandana is ¥600 a day. Only beach and ropeway lots charge ¥1,000–2,000.
  • Never on the shoulder at Shirahige Shrine or That Bench. Both get patrolled.
Toll notes
  • ETC takes 30 percent off on weekends and holidays, and 30 percent off between midnight and 04:00 on weekdays. The discount is suspended during Silver Week.
  • The Chūbu-Jūkan Expressway between Takayama and Hida-Kiyomi, and the Kyoto-Jūkan section around Kameoka, are toll-free.
  • The foreign-visitor Central Nippon Expressway Pass was discontinued in 2026; there is no flat-rate pass for the Alps route.

Eating vegetarian

Rural Japan hides fish in the stock. These are the safety nets.

Every lunch in the plans names a checked vegetarian kitchen. For everything in between, show this at the counter:

私はベジタリアンです。肉・魚・魚のだし・かつおぶしは食べられません。卵と乳製品は大丈夫です。“I am vegetarian. I cannot eat meat, fish, fish stock or bonito flakes. Egg and dairy are fine.” Drop the last sentence if you are vegan.
これは肉や魚のだしが入っていますか？“Does this contain meat or fish stock?” The answer you want is 入っていません, haitte imasen.
Chains that always work
  • CoCo Ichibanya lists a Vegetarian Curry made without animal ingredients, at almost every expressway exit town, open until 23:00.
  • Mos Burger Green Burger is plant-based; skip the sauce if strict.
  • Convenience stores: ume, kombu and natto-maki onigiri, inari-zushi, edamame, tofu, fruit. Egg sandwiches are vegetarian; tuna and “sea chicken” are not.
  • Michi-no-eki roadside stations sell local tofu, pickles, mountain vegetables and fruit: picnic material for Kamikōchi and Ōdaigahara.
Near home, after a drive
  • Imakoko Kitchen Merrymomo, 6-8-8 Nishinakajima. Macrobiotic set meals, 7:30–21:00, closed Sundays and holidays. The closest proper vegetarian dinner to the flat.
  • Vegecafe+α, 6-9-20 Nishinakajima. Vegetable plates and cake, 10:00–17:00, Wednesday to Sunday. Pre-drive brunch.
  • Paprika Shokudō Vegan, Shinsaibashi. Soy karaage, pasta, big salads, open late.
  • Green Earth, Honmachi. Osaka’s oldest vegetarian restaurant; cheap curry and sandwiches.
Temple food, booked ahead

Both plans stay within reach of shōjin ryōri, the Buddhist vegetarian cuisine: Kōyasan in Wakayama and Enryaku-ji on Mount Hiei serve it at lunch. A ryokan in Hirayu will cook a meat-free dinner if you write ベジタリアン料理希望 in the booking notes three days ahead.

Worth adding

Nature within thirty minutes of the route that was not on the list.

September

Light, weather, temperature, colour.

Light

Sunset 18:15 on the 1st, 17:50 by the 30th. Yuhigaura and That Bench are light-dependent; be parked forty minutes before.

Weather

Typhoon season. Check the JMA forecast two days out; if one is tracking toward Kansai, swap the coast or Alps day for Ōdaigahara’s short walk. Ōdaigahara itself is one of the wettest places in Japan: go in the morning.

Temperature

Osaka still 30 °C. Kamikōchi mornings 8–12 °C, the Norikura summit 5 °C with wind. Fleece, rain shell, closed shoes, even for the day trips.

Colour

Hirugano’s 6,000 kochia bushes turn red from late September. Norikura’s alpine leaves begin in the last week. The Metasequoia avenue stays green until November.
