What September normally does. Osaka gets about 150 mm of rain over ten wet days in September and stays near 30 °C. The Tango coast and Lake Biwa see roughly 180–200 mm. Ōdaigahara is one of the wettest places in Japan, close to 600 mm in September alone, and it clouds over most afternoons, so it is a morning stop. Typhoons are the wild card: if one is forecast to approach Kansai, swap the mountain day for the lake day or stay home; the Ōdaigahara Driveway and coastal Route 178 close in warnings.

The four Alpine places on your list

Kamikōchi, Mount Norikura, Hirayu Waterfall and Hirugano Plateau are five to six hours’ drive from Osaka and the first two ban private cars, so they cannot be day trips. They make a superb separate three-day loop with two nights in Hirayu Onsen: Osaka → Hirugano → Hirayu, a shuttle day in Kamikōchi, then the Norikura Skyline bus and home via Takayama. Keep them for a long weekend when you can sleep away from Hōshin.

Essentials

The car, the money, the food

The car

Every rental in Japan is automatic. Book a compact hybrid (Yaris, Aqua, Fit or Note class): about ¥6,000–8,500 a day in September, 25 km per litre, fine on mountain roads. A kei car is ¥1,500 cheaper and slower on the climbs.

  • Toyota Rent a Car Higashi-Mikuni, 3-10-21 Higashimikuni. Eight minutes by taxi from Hōshin; the closest desk.
  • Times, ORIX or Toyota at Shin-Osaka, twelve minutes by Metro. Times and ORIX are usually cheapest on web rates.
  • Compare on Tabirai Japan or ToCoo!; add an ETC card (¥330) and the NOC waiver (¥550 a day).
  • Bring passport, home licence and a 1949 Geneva Convention International Driving Permit. Singapore and Malaysia permits are accepted. Return with a full tank.
  • Pick up Thursday 08:00, return Saturday by 20:00. Keep the car all three nights; coin parking near Kami-Shinjō caps at ¥800–1,200 a night (look for 夜間最大), or pre-book a space on akippa.

The food

Each day names a checked vegetarian kitchen for lunch. Rural Japan hides fish in the stock, so for everything else show this:

私はベジタリアンです。肉・魚・魚のだし・かつおぶしは食べられません。“I am vegetarian. I cannot eat meat, fish, fish stock or bonito flakes.”
  • CoCo Ichibanya has a Vegetarian Curry with no animal ingredients, in almost every town, open until 23:00.
  • Convenience stores: ume, kombu and natto onigiri, inari-zushi, edamame, tofu, fruit. Tuna and “sea chicken” are fish.
  • Near home: Imakoko Kitchen Merrymomo (6-8-8 Nishinakajima, macrobiotic, until 21:00, closed Sundays and holidays) and Vegecafe+α (6-9-20 Nishinakajima, 10:00–17:00, Wed–Sun).
