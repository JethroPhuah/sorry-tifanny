Bring to the counter

  • Passport, home driving licence, and a 1949 Geneva Convention International Driving Permit. Singapore (AA Singapore, same-day) and Malaysia (JPJ) permits are accepted.
  • If your licence is from Germany, France, Switzerland, Belgium, Monaco, Taiwan, Slovenia or Estonia you need a JAF Japanese translation instead of an IDP.
  • Ask for an ETC card (about ¥330 for the rental). It saves 30 percent on many tolls and is the only way through the Hirugano Kōgen smart interchange.
  • Add the NOC waiver (about ¥550 a day). Without it a scratch costs ¥20,000–50,000 on top of insurance.
  • Return with a full tank (say “mantan”), and keep the receipt.

Parking at home and on the road

  • Overnight near Hōshin. Coin parking around Kami-Shinjō and Awaji stations caps at roughly ¥800–1,200 per night (look for 夜間最大 “night maximum” on the sign). The akippa app pre-books private spaces from about ¥500 a day, and Times Parking has several lots within 300 m of the address. Ask whether the building has a visitor bay first.
  • Plan B keeps the car three days and parks at home each night. Returning to the desk nightly costs more in time than the parking does.
  • Mountain lots are cheap or free. Ōdaigahara, Kōyasan-style temple lots, Hirayu Falls, Honokidaira and Makino Pic-Land are free. Akandana (Kamikōchi) is ¥600 a day. Only beach and ropeway lots charge ¥1,000–2,000.
  • Park facing out in tight lots, and never on the shoulder at Shirahige Shrine or That Bench; both get patrolled.

Eating vegetarian in the mountains

Rural Japan hides fish in dashi stock. The stops above name a checked vegetarian place for every lunch; this is the safety net for everything else.

Show this at any counter

私はベジタリアンです。肉・魚・魚のだし・かつおぶしは食べられません。卵と乳製品は大丈夫です。“I am vegetarian. I cannot eat meat, fish, fish stock or bonito flakes. Egg and dairy are fine.” Delete the last sentence if you are vegan.
これは肉や魚のだしが入っていますか？“Does this contain meat or fish stock?” Answer to hope for: 入っていません (haitte imasen, “it does not”).

Chains that always work

  • CoCo Ichibanya curry houses list a “Vegetarian Curry” made without animal ingredients; there is one at almost every expressway exit town and they open until 23:00.
  • Mos Burger Green Burger is plant-based; ask for it without the sauce if you are strict.
  • Convenience stores: ume, kombu and natto-maki onigiri, inari-zushi, edamame, tofu, fruit cups, salted rice crackers. Egg sandwiches are vegetarian; tuna and “sea chicken” are not.
  • Michi-no-eki roadside stations sell local tofu, pickles, mountain vegetables (sansai) and fresh fruit. Good picnic material for Kamikōchi and Ōdaigahara.

Near home, for dinner after a drive

  • Imakoko Kitchen Merrymomo, 6-8-8 Nishinakajima. Macrobiotic set meals, open 7:30–21:00, closed Sundays and holidays. Ten minutes from Shin-Osaka; the closest proper vegetarian dinner to the flat.
  • Vegecafe+α, 6-9-20 Nishinakajima. Vegetable plates and cake, 10:00–17:00, Wednesday to Sunday. Good for a pre-drive brunch, not dinner.
  • Paprika Shokudō Vegan, Shinsaibashi. Soy karaage, pasta, big salads; open late.
  • Green Earth, Honmachi. Osaka’s oldest vegetarian restaurant, cheap curry and sandwiches.

Book ahead for temple food

Both plans stay within reach of shōjin ryōri, the Buddhist vegetarian cuisine: Kōyasan (Wakayama) and Enryaku-ji on Mount Hiei (Shiga) serve it at lunch. A ryokan in Hirayu will cook a meat-free dinner if you write “ベジタリアン料理希望” in the booking notes at least three days ahead.

September specifics

Light

Sunset 18:15 on 1 Sept, 17:50 by 30 Sept. Yuhigaura and That Bench are sunset stops; be parked forty minutes before.

Weather

Typhoon season. Check the JMA forecast two days out; if a typhoon is tracking toward Kansai, swap the Alps or coast day for Ōdaigahara’s shorter walk. Ōdaigahara is one of the wettest places in Japan, so go in the morning.

Temperature

Osaka lowlands still 30 °C. Kamikōchi mornings 8–12 °C, Norikura summit 5 °C with wind. Pack a fleece, a rain shell and closed shoes even for the day trips.

Seasonal colour

Hirugano’s 6,000 kochia bushes turn red from late September. Norikura’s alpine leaves start turning in the last week. Metasequoia is still green; it turns copper in November.
